Support

For help with Plank Timer, email gtcappstudio@gmail.com. Include your iOS version, app version, selected language, and the steps that led to the issue.

Common questions

Does my timer continue in the background?

Yes. The timer reconciles with elapsed time when you return. iOS can pause visual updates while another app is active, but the session deadline remains accurate.

Where is my history stored?

On your iPhone. Plank Timer version 1.0 has no login or cloud sync, so deleting the app also deletes its locally stored history.

Why did an early stop not update my streak?

Attempts of at least five seconds appear in history and statistics. Only completed goals advance the streak. Very short taps are ignored.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Open Plank Timer → Settings → Ad privacy options when that item is shown. You can also manage the iOS tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Report an inappropriate ad

Send a report to gtcappstudio@gmail.com. If possible, include:

  • a screenshot of the ad;
  • the date, time, and country where it appeared;
  • whether it was a bottom banner or full-screen ad; and
  • the advertiser name or destination shown after tapping the ad.

Do not include sensitive personal or health information. Reports help us identify the placement and escalate inappropriate creative to the advertising provider.

Fitness safety

Plank Timer is a timing utility, not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort, and consult a qualified health professional before starting a new exercise program.
