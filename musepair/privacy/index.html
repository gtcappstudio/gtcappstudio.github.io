Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026 · MusePair iOS beta

MusePair is operated by Goktug Cinar under GTC App Studio. For privacy questions or requests, contact gtcappstudio@gmail.com.

Information you provide

We store your account ID, email address, and any name you provide. Apple or Google sign-in shares the identity information you authorize; Apple may provide a private-relay email address.

We store photos you upload, their garment cutouts and previews, garment information and your corrections, saved outfits, and optional body-profile photos and generated try-ons. Optional preferences can include gender identity, age range, colors, seasons, occasions, and style preferences.

We also process recommendation display and refresh history, job status, and technical records needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. If you contact support, we receive your message and any attachments you choose to send.

How we use it

This information lets us maintain and synchronize your wardrobe, provide outfit suggestions, process requested images, respond to support requests, and prevent abuse. The app caches images and wardrobe information on your device for faster browsing. It does not contain advertising SDKs or use your data for cross-app advertising tracking.

Photo processing and providers

Some photo preparation and extraction runs on your device. Uploaded photos and app records are also stored and processed in the cloud. Supabase provides authentication, database, and storage; Railway hosts our API and background processing. Apple and Google handle their respective sign-in services.

When you request virtual try-on, your selected body photo and garment images are sent to OpenAI to generate the preview. OpenAI’s standard API policy does not use API content for model training unless the API customer explicitly opts in. Its standard abuse-monitoring records can be retained for up to 30 days, with longer retention for legal or safety exceptions. See OpenAI’s data controls. Deleting a MusePair image does not immediately erase provider-held security records.

Apple processes TestFlight installation, usage, diagnostics, and feedback under its own terms and privacy policy, and makes beta feedback and relevant diagnostics available to us. Our support email is handled through Google’s email service. Service providers may process data in countries other than where you live.

Sharing

We do not automatically publish your wardrobe. If you create a share link or export an image, recipients may view, save, or forward the shared result. We also disclose information to the providers above to deliver the service and when required by law or necessary to protect people and the service. We do not sell your wardrobe photos.

Retention and deletion

Account and wardrobe data remains in the active service while needed to provide your account, unless you delete it. Recommendations use a rolling 30-day exposure window; older recommendation records are cleaned up during subsequent recommendation activity. Operational records, backups, and provider security records may remain for their applicable retention periods and legal obligations.

You can correct garment details and delete garments or body photos in the app. Profile includes Delete Account And All Data, which removes your sign-in account and associated active-service wardrobe records and uploaded photos. Apple-linked accounts may require fresh authorization to revoke Apple’s connection. Deletion cannot recall copies other people saved from your exports or shared links.

Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, including if you cannot sign in. We may verify account ownership before acting. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, including the right to contact your local data-protection authority.

Your choices

You can use closet and outfit features without a body profile. iOS Settings lets you manage camera and photo access. Upload only photos you have permission to use; use your own clothed body photo for try-on. The close-circle beta is not intended for children.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies to these pages.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change. Contact the address above with any questions.
