Getting started

How do I join the beta?

MusePair is currently being prepared for close-circle testing. There is no public TestFlight link. If you have been invited, follow the invitation from the developer in Apple’s TestFlight app.

Do I need a body photo?

No. Your closet and saved outfits work without one. Add a front body photo only if you want to use virtual try-on.

Can I correct a garment?

Open the item in Closet and use its editing controls to update its name, category, colors, and other available details. Photo extraction and automatic tags can make mistakes.

Why does a try-on take time?

Try-ons are generated online. Keep an internet connection and return to the outfit to check its result. If processing fails or seems stuck, send us the approximate time and what you tried—there is no need to repeatedly request another image.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete Account And All Data. This removes your account and associated active-service data. Apple-linked accounts may ask you to authorize with Apple again to disconnect. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account; we will verify ownership before handling a deletion request. See the privacy policy for retention details.

TestFlight feedback

You can also send feedback through TestFlight. Please avoid including other people’s photos or personal information in reports. AI previews are for outfit exploration and do not measure your body or guarantee clothing fit.
